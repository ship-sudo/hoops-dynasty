## Gaps

- Salaries 1998..2020 have no player ids in the source; 916 of 11690 rows stay unmatched after name matching. Those need the id_map override file. "extra" is 0 for csv rows by construction (ids only come from stints).
- Salaries 2014 are short in the source: 412 rows vs 450+ in neighbouring seasons. One NA-salary row (1998+) is dropped.
- Salaries 2021..2026 come from the bref contracts page, which lists standard contracts only: two-way, Exhibit 10 and 10-day deals are absent. See "covered %" above for how many players with minutes are missing.
- The 2025 snapshot (2024-10-07) is 15 days before opening night; the next one (2024-11-15) is 24 days after. Nearest was chosen.
- Contract length and options before 2021 are not in any free source; only 2021+ snapshots carry option flags. The current page gives 2026-27 onward.
- Award Shares and End of Season Teams stop at 2025; the 2026 awards were not in the repo at its 2026-04-13 update. All-Star Selections has 2026.
- Draft Pick History stops at the 2025 draft; the 2026 draft is missing for the same reason.
- Team Per 100 and Opp Per 100 have no League Average row (29/30 rows per season vs 30/31 in the other team files).
- Player Play By Play and Player Shooting start in 1997 in the source, so 1998+ is complete.
- Luxury tax, aprons, min/max salary and rookie scale are not in these files. Hand-curated era table is a separate lane.
